BeforeGet ready

Nothing to install. Do these before the mapathon, so you can start mapping as soon as you arrive.

  • Make a free OpenStreetMap account

    Sign up at openstreetmap.org, then click the link in your confirmation email. This is the step that slows people down most on the day. Your username and edits are public, so your username doesn't have to be your real name.

  • Make sure you can log in to the HOT Tasking Manager

    Go to tasks.hotosm.org, click Log in, and sign in with your new OpenStreetMap account. If your username shows at the top right, you're all set. If it doesn't work, check that you confirmed your email, or ask a helper when you arrive.

  • Optional: try the practice walkthrough

    On openstreetmap.org, click Edit, then Help›Start the walkthrough. It takes about 10 minutes, and nothing you do in it is saved to the real map.

Day ofDay of the mapathon

  • Bring your laptop, and a mouse if you have one

    Any laptop with a web browser works. A mouse makes tracing much easier. No laptop? We have Chromebooks available.

  • Sign in at the table

    Then pick up a cheat sheet. It has everything below, plus shortcuts and fixes for common snags.

  • Scan the survey code on the table

    It takes a minute and helps the library keep running these sessions.

DuringAdd to the Map

Work at your own pace, and raise your hand whenever you need a helper. Haven't tried the practice walkthrough yet? Start there: on openstreetmap.org, click Edit, then Help›Start the walkthrough.

  1. Pick a square

    Log in at tasks.hotosm.org. Open this month's project, check its number, read the instructions, pick an available square, then Map›iD Editor.

  2. Trace in iD

    Trace only what the instructions ask for, inside your square, from the satellite imagery. Most projects ask for buildings; some ask for roads, or both.

  3. Save and submit

    Save, keep the project hashtag, add #AUmapathon and a few words, then Upload. In the Tasking Manager, mark the square completely mapped, or choose Stop mapping. Validators check every square.

  • Before you leave: take the exit survey

    Scan the exit survey code on the table. It takes a minute.
