# Library Mapathon

**Date:** Monthly, starting 2026 (staff pilot: November 11, 2026)
**Hosted by:** Digital Research and Innovation Lab, American University Library
**Instructor:** Jessica Breen, PhD
**Tags:** OpenStreetMap, humanitarian mapping, HOT Tasking Manager, iD editor, open data, GIS

## Abstract

A free, three-hour drop-in session held once a month. Participants learn to map features such as buildings and roads from satellite imagery in OpenStreetMap and contribute to a humanitarian mapping project coordinated by the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team. After setting up an account, they work through three self-paced steps: pick a square in the Tasking Manager, trace what the project asks for in the iD editor, and save and submit with a documented changeset comment. No mapping or GIS experience is needed.

## Materials

- `index.html`: participant website (GitHub Pages), organized into tabs for each workshop section
- `slides/`: slides with speaker notes (`slides.html`, `slides.pdf`)
- `handouts/`: two-page cheat sheet (the three mapping steps with checkpoints, shortcuts, snags) and On your own guide (HTML and PDF)
- `materials/mapathon-materials.zip`: the printable handouts in one download
- `instructor/`: Facilitator Guide (generated from the slide notes) and checkpoint reference
- `_variables.yml` and the THIS MONTH blocks in `index.html`: the details that change each month
- `scripts/build.sh`: re-renders everything; `scripts/make_guide.py`: rebuilds the Facilitator Guide

## Topics Covered

- Humanitarian mapping, OpenStreetMap and HOT, and how they relate
- Mapping responsibly from satellite imagery
- Choosing and locking a task in the HOT Tasking Manager
- Mapping what a project's instructions ask for in the iD editor, usually buildings or roads
- Saving with a changeset comment and hashtag as a record of the work
- Finishing or releasing a task for validation

## Partners / Series

Library Mapathon, a monthly series. November sessions can double as the library's GIS Day event. Projects come from the HOT Tasking Manager.
